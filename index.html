Important Notice

Medical & Regulatory Disclaimer

Clinical Decision Support Only. BoneScan X is designed as an assistive tool for qualified healthcare professionals. It does not replace clinical judgment, and all outputs must be reviewed by a licensed radiologist or physician before being used for patient diagnosis or treatment.

Not a Standalone Diagnostic. The AI-generated findings, confidence scores, and visual overlays are probabilistic in nature and may produce false positives or false negatives. Final diagnostic authority rests with the attending clinician.

Regulatory Status. BoneScan X is currently in clinical validation and is not yet FDA-cleared or CE-marked for autonomous diagnostic use. Deployment is limited to research, pilot, and supervised clinical environments.

Data & Privacy. All patient imaging processed through BoneScan X is handled in accordance with HIPAA, GDPR, and applicable local data-protection regulations. No patient data is used for model training without explicit, documented consent.
